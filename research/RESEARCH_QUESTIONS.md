# Candidate Wayfarer Research Questions

These are research candidates, not a locked thesis proposal.

## Current strongest umbrella question

> To what extent can a renderer-agnostic subject state preserve a simulated individual's recognizable identity and developmental continuity across language-model substitutions and host transfers, and which state components are causally necessary to that preservation?

This question fits Wayfarer's central architecture without assuming in advance that every existing mechanism is necessary or sufficient.

## Candidate subquestions

### Model substitution and identity invariance

How much behavioral and expressive continuity can be preserved when the generative model changes while canonical character state remains fixed?

Useful distinction:

- **behavioral continuity**: beliefs, relationships, commitments, memory-grounded decisions, developmental trajectory;
- **expressive continuity**: style, directness, verbosity, humor, hedging, initiative, conversational rhythm, and other recognizable surface features.

### Causally sufficient present

Can a bounded active subject state preserve longitudinal behavior while an arbitrarily larger autobiography remains cold and recoverable?

This should compare bounded causal availability against broader or full-context autobiographical retrieval rather than treating resident-memory count as the independent variable.

### Path-dependent development

Are developmental outcomes determined only by accumulated evidence, or do the sequence and consolidation boundaries through which experience was processed alter later subject state?

Wayfarer's existing slow-belief results make this a concrete, falsifiable direction.

### Cross-host continuity

Which invariants are necessary to distinguish `same individual moved` from `copied descendant with shared past` across host migration?

The current shared-store writer handoff is only an initial case. Disconnected copies and explicit branching remain a stronger future test.

### Minimal mechanism

What is the smallest set of state families and causal mechanisms that preserves the target continuity properties under ablation?

This is closer to Wayfarer's design philosophy than asking whether a large cognitive architecture can imitate human cognition.

## Candidate comparative hypotheses

These are not established findings:

- model-agnostic subject state should preserve behavioral identity better than prompt/history transfer alone;
- model-specific renderer adapters may preserve expressive identity better than a single universal persona prompt;
- bounded causally relevant memory may outperform broad resident autobiography under distractor pressure;
- replay that preserves developmental boundaries should preserve later behavior better than replay of inputs alone;
- explicit custody/branch semantics should reduce false claims that two diverged copies are one continuing subject.

## Do not lock the thesis too early

The implementation should continue to be falsified on engineering grounds. A later thesis should select the question for which Wayfarer has the strongest reproducible evidence, meaningful comparison conditions, and defensible independent evaluation.
