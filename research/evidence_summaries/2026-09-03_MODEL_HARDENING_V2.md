This is evidence about a demonstrated prompt-induced failure, not proof of universal expressive range.

The scope of unsupported interpretation is narrower than an indiscriminate ban on speculation. Confident unsupported motive assertions and claimed unavailable memories are different cases. Renderer interpretations do not become canonical biography.

That is a bounded capability limitation, not a successful raw model answer. Preserving partial knowledge is preferable to inventing a color. Both characters' selected acts and canonical projections remain the reference; model fluency never chooses the expected decision.
