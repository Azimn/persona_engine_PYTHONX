# Literature and Related-Work Notes

This directory contains research notes and scholarly comparisons. These files may include tentative interpretations, bibliographic leads, secondary-source material, and ideas for future experiments.

They are not implementation requirements.

`RESOURCES_FOR_SCHOLARLY_WORK.md` was moved here from the repository root without changing its blob contents so the original research note remains intact while the repository gains a clear research/engineering boundary.

`ADJACENT_CHARACTER_ARCHITECTURES_2026-09-02.md` records a comparative review of recent character-agent research and open-source companion systems against Wayfarer's current architecture. It separates transferable mechanisms from explicit non-adoptions and defines candidate experiments for temporal propositions, appraisal, subjective encoding, associative retrieval, speech delivery receipts, witness scope, and long-horizon evaluation. Runtime experiments from that review must remain separate from the frozen Phase D actual-model collection until that evidence is captured or deliberately re-versioned.
