# Dyadic Rituals

A dyadic ritual is a repeated structured interaction pattern for one
relationship, such as work-interruption acknowledgment or returning to an open
loop. It stores action kind, communicative function, trigger pattern, context,
strength, repetition counts, and evidence IDs. It never stores generated
sentences.

Three structurally similar repetitions are required for support. A supported
ritual enters synthesis and may shape action only through the canonical action
resolver. It cannot bypass current pressure, intention, identity, safety, or
World Authority.
