# Relationship Expectations

Relationship expectations are character-owned subjective regularities such as
`returns_to_open_loops`. They reuse `IdentityLedger.per_relationship_beliefs`
and do not create objective facts about the user.

An expectation requires at least three supporting episodes across two days
and confidence of at least `0.65` before becoming `usually`. Violations weaken
confidence gradually; one violation does not reset an established pattern.
Expectations enter synthesis as bounded relationship evidence.
