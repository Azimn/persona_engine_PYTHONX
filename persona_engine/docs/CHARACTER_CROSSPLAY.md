# Character Crossplay

Kiki and Pretorius can test one another through isolated `CharacterAgent`
instances and separate SQLite databases. Only observable speech, action, and
host-confirmed consequences cross the boundary. Memory, self-monitor state,
synthesis, beliefs, pressures, and relationship history never cross.

Available scenarios cover Kiki testing Pretorius, Pretorius testing Kiki, and
alternating 30-day crossplay. Crossplay provides richer pressure than a fixed
script, but it cannot replace scripted human policies because two characters
may develop a closed ecology that hides human-facing defects.
